Interpretation
--------------

This trace does not show memory exhaustion or a failed ioremap: the FACS mapping routine is not called. The local EFI ACPI generator in src/vm/IA64AcpiFirmware.cpp emits an RSDT and XSDT with a single MADT entry and has no FADT, FACS, or DSDT builder.
