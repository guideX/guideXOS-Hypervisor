Interpretation
- Early acpi_enable_subsystem entered and returned AE_OK. acpi_enable returned
  AE_OK and ACPICA reported ACPI mode. FACS initialization was explicitly
  disabled for this early call by ACPI_NO_FACS_INIT.
- The later bus call with ACPI_NO_ACPI_ENABLE (bit 4 set, bit 7 clear) is the
  call that can initialize FACS; it was not observed because acpi_init did not run.
- Outcome B: lifecycle cause explained, authentic FACS initialization/mapping
  pending. The R28 failure chain was not reproduced, but its permanent
  elimination and successful ACPI bus initialization are not established.
