Conclusion
  The level-4 table slot points to an IA-64 function descriptor, not directly
  to the callback code address. Its descriptor resolves exactly to the mapped
  acpi_init symbol. Table ordering places it after topology_init4 and before
  dock_init4, within level 4 (the subsystem initcall section), and before the
  level-5 dma_init5 marker. Runtime reachability/call/return are established
  separately by r31-probe-events.txt; these static bytes alone do not prove
  that Linux's walker reached this slot.
