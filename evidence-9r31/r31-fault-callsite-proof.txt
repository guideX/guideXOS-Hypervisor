Observed disposition and limits
  The kthreadd task enters die() at cycle 835,611,662 and do_exit at cycle
  836,295,972 with argument 11; its state becomes TASK_DEAD, then scheduler
  selects init_task. This establishes the immediate bad pointer and faulting copy.
  The trace does not show how p->mempolicy acquired 0xffff0000, nor does it log
  the kthread_create_info object/name. The source and scheduler timeline strongly
  correlate the fault with the default_bdi_init4 kthread_run wait, but do not
  prove request-object pointer equality. No CPU, firmware, or guest change was made.
