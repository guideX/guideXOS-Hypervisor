guest MMIO write trace after each CPU step. Retained records contain global
byte order, UART identity and offset, byte value, MMIO sequence/address/
register/width, guest PC, privilege, post-handoff retirement count, handoff
origin, and SBI association. Capacity is bounded at 16,384 events.
