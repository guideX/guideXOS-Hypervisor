Scope and unresolved point
  R32 proves the source bytes, child overwrite, and exact first incorrect
  guest-memory transition. It proves no independent stale policy object,
  allocator lifetime error, or incoming malformed parent pointer.
  The underlying emulator semantic that makes the authentic software-pipelined
  memcpy place the neighboring qword at the child field is not yet isolated
  between the RRBGR/rotating-GR mapping and rotating-predicate pipeline stages.
  The general fault is not repaired speculatively. The next diagnostic should
  run a small exact 25-stage load/store loop with observable p16/p40 and
  rotating-GR stage values, compare each cycle with Intel's architectural
  semantics, then add a focused regression before changing execution code.
